# TRACE strengthening after the KBS concern audit

This local amendment is recorded on 11 October 2026 (Asia/Shanghai), after the
original and v3 outcomes and the KBS rejection comments are known. It is not a
public preregistration, an unseen-outcome test or a replacement for any frozen
experiment. All new files use v4 namespaces. Existing model fits, predictions,
decisions, label vaults, public releases and frozen reviewers are preserved.

## Questions and modules

1. Does the allocation-matched conclusion extend to a separately pretrained T5
   encoder family trained on the exact v3 source roles? Three actual seed fits
   retain fixed source-only checkpoint selection. The complete target cohorts
   remain separate. Available commit/repository/clone/CVE/date metadata determine
   which additional isolation statements can be tested. Missing fields do not
   license invented chronology or vulnerability-event independence.
2. Which officially implemented PROM operations can run on the binary detector
   outputs, and what workload, automatic-label errors and scenario costs result?
   The official repository and exact source revision are pinned. Any adaptation,
   omitted expert workflow or incremental detector retraining is disclosed.
3. How do limited review capacity and simulated delayed review feedback alter
   actions and costs? New capacities and update rules are fixed before computing
   outcomes. Actual timestamp fields are distinguished from synthetic order.
   Reviewed items alone supply delayed feedback unless another labelled scenario
   explicitly accounts for the cost of obtaining additional labels.
4. In controlled distributions where the covariate ratio and conditional-label
   relation are known, how do estimation, clipping and conditional shift affect
   weighted conformal decisions? These simulations diagnose assumptions and do
   not certify real software-project coverage.

Each module records its own exact executable configuration and protocol hashes
before its first new outcome comparison. The root records a protocol-clearance
receipt. New source-only feasibility/pilot activity is identified separately
and cannot tune settings to target outcomes. GPU use is serialized by the root.

## Endpoints, units and transparency

Core software comparisons retain automatic-safe, automatic-vulnerable and review
counts; missed-vulnerability and false-alert rates use the complete true-class
denominators. Error conditional on automation is undefined at zero automation.
All policy outcomes, including failures and unfavorable directions, are retained.
Actual seeds are averaged within each project before paired project summaries.
Collections are not pooled; shared names and source objects are not replication.
Any secondary capacity, delay, budget or clip family is fully retained and
descriptive. No target-optimal parameter, subset, threshold or cost is selected.

Real security-team review time, error, feedback latency and remediation benefit
are not observed. Simulated feedback and costs must be called simulated. Source
metadata without dates cannot establish source-before-target temporal novelty.
No local check, replay record or synthetic experiment guarantees vulnerability
truth, deployment safety, generalization or journal acceptance.

## Evidence delivery

New code, configs, actual execution/training receipts, independent checks and
aggregate tables are retained. Raw functions, weights, row predictions and label
vaults remain local. Manuscript changes cite performed analyses only, preserve
negative evidence and keep the center on allocation and operational evaluation.
Public release and journal submission are separate actions; this amendment does
not authorize contacting others or answering unprovided portal attestations.
